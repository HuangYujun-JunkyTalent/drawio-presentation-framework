Contributions are welcome as issues and as pull requests.

A contribution needs no signed agreement and no sign-off line.
A contribution comes under the license of this repository, the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3, in `LICENSE`, with the additional permission in `LICENSE-EXCEPTION.md`.
The terms of service of GitHub say the same of every contribution to a repository with a license, in their section D.6.
The contributor keeps the copyright of the contribution.

By opening a pull request, you confirm that you have the right to give the contribution under these terms.
